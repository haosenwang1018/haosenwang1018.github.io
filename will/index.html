这份遗书写给还活着的我。

我这一生真正重要的事如下：

  1. 保持身体健康、精神清醒、意志强韧。
  2. 和我现在爱的伴侣一直走下去。珍惜她，忠诚于她，和她共同度过这一生。
  3. 对所有在生物学和社会学上帮助过我的人抱有感恩，以行动为准。
  4. 在 AGI 实现之前，为 AGI 做出卓越贡献。
  5. 在 AGI 实现之前，为人类做出卓越贡献。
  6. 把智能带入空间，为人类走向深空的真实能力做出卓越贡献。
  7. 后 AGI 时代，为人类维持尊严、保留主权作出贡献。
  8. 在中国打败美国，或者在中国被美国打败之前，为我的土地做出卓越贡献。
  9. 让世界没有战争，或者至少，让战争更难发生。为宇宙和平作出卓越贡献。

This will is written to the version of me who is still alive.

The things that truly matter in my life are these:

  1. Keep my body healthy, my mind clear, and my will strong.
  2. Walk through life with the partner I love now. Cherish her, be loyal to her, and spend this life with her.
  3. Be grateful to everyone who has helped me biologically and socially, and let action be the proof.
  4. Before AGI is achieved, make an extraordinary contribution to AGI.
  5. Before AGI is achieved, make an extraordinary contribution to humanity.
  6. Bring intelligence into space, and make an extraordinary contribution to humanity's real ability to reach deep space.
  7. In the post-AGI era, contribute to preserving human dignity and sovereignty.
  8. Before China defeats America, or before China is defeated by America, make an extraordinary contribution to my land.
  9. Make a world without war; or at least, make war harder to happen. Make an extraordinary contribution to peace in the universe.
